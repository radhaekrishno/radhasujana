The trail we took.

A few waypoints from our Annapurna honeymoon. The longer story is still finding its way into our journal.

    A public bus, and the beginning of our honeymoon on foot.

  2. Birethanti → Ghandruk

    The checkpost, then a mix of walking and bike rides to Ghandruk.

  3. Ghandruk → Jhinu → Chomrong

    A jeep to Jhinu, across the suspension bridge and uphill to our first mountain stay.

  4. Chomrong → Dovan → Machhapuchhre Base Camp

    Through the valley, past waterfalls, and into the high mountains.

  5. Annapurna Base Camp · 17 September 2026

    We made it. A birthday, our honeymoon, and a memory we get to keep.

  6. ABC → Bamboo → Jhinu → Pokhara

    Down the trail, a night in Bamboo, and the jeep journey back to Pokhara.
